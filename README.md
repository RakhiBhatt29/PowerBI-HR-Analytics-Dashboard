# Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Description

The Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence project designed to analyze employee data, workforce composition, and employee attrition.

The dashboard converts HR data into meaningful insights using KPIs, interactive visualizations, slicers, and cross-filtering. It helps understand employee demographics, attrition patterns, salary levels, job roles, departments, and work experience.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall employee strength and active workforce.
- Monitor employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Understand attrition across departments and salary slabs.
- Analyze employee demographics by age and gender.
- Identify patterns across job roles and satisfaction levels.
- Examine the relationship between experience and attrition.
- Compare employee distribution across departments.
- Enable interactive HR analysis using filters.

## Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared using Power Query by:

- Checking and cleaning employee records.
- Handling inconsistent or missing values where required.
- Removing duplicate records where applicable.
- Correcting data types and categorical values.
- Creating required fields such as Age Group and Salary Slab.
- Preparing the dataset for HR analysis and visualization.

## Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks important HR metrics including:

- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Experience

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the organization's workforce and employee retention situation.

## Dashboard Analysis

### Attrition by Department

Shows how employee attrition is distributed across departments.

**Business Use:**
- Identify departments experiencing higher employee exits.
- Compare workforce retention across departments.
- Support department-level HR analysis.

### Attrition by Salary Slab

Analyzes employee attrition across different salary ranges.

**Business Use:**
- Understand whether attrition varies across compensation levels.
- Identify salary groups requiring further investigation.
- Support compensation and retention analysis.

### Attrition by Job Role & Satisfaction

Uses a matrix to analyze attrition across different job roles and satisfaction levels.

**Business Use:**
- Identify roles with higher employee exits.
- Understand the relationship between job satisfaction and attrition.
- Support targeted employee-retention strategies.

### Age Group Distribution

Shows the distribution of employees across different age groups.

**Business Use:**
- Understand workforce demographics.
- Identify the organization's major employee age segments.
- Support workforce planning.

### Attrition by Gender

Compares employee attrition between male and female employees.

**Business Use:**
- Analyze gender-wise workforce and attrition patterns.
- Support diversity and workforce analysis.

### Attrition Trend by Experience

Visualizes attrition across different levels of employee experience.

**Business Use:**
- Identify experience groups with higher attrition.
- Understand employee retention patterns across career stages.
- Support onboarding and retention analysis.

### Department-Wise Employee Count

Compares the number of employees across departments.

**Business Use:**
- Understand workforce distribution.
- Identify departments with larger employee populations.
- Support resource and workforce planning.

## Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

- Department
- Age Group
- Gender

Users can select different categories and dynamically analyze how the HR metrics and visualizations change.
